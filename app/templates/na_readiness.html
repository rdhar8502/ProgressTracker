🇺🇸 US FAANG & High-Scale Tech (Google, Meta, Amazon, Netflix, Stripe)
Algorithmic Speed & Ultra-Scale Design: Expect intense 45-min coding rounds where you must produce bug-free, optimal O(N) or O(log N) solutions in 25 mins. System Design demands 1M+ QPS sharding, cache stampede mitigation, consensus (Raft/Paxos), and back-of-the-envelope storage math. Amazon Bar Raiser enforces rigorous STAR behavioral answers backed by hard business metrics.
🇨🇦 Canadian Tech Hubs (Shopify, 1Password, Amazon/Google Canada)
Craftsmanship, Paired Design & Pragmatism: Shopify and 1Password emphasize the "Life Story" interview, paired code review/refactoring, and real-world system resilience. Evaluates how you handle technical debt, Black Friday traffic spikes, asynchronous event pipelines (Kafka), and cross-functional team empathy.

Third-Party Vendor Payroll & Client Placement Model (AllianceTek / Denali)
Staff Augmentation, Vendor Middleman Payroll & US Client Contractor Reality
🤝 Third-Party Payroll 💵 $75k–$105k / $40–$55/hr
Third-Party Vendor Payroll Mechanics
• Middleman Staffing Structure: AllianceTek / Denali operates as the vendor partner, placing engineers onto US client projects on third-party payroll.
• Vendor Margin: The end client bills at standard US contractor rates ($65–$85/hr); the staffing agency retains a 30%–45% margin.
• Realistic Candidate Take-Home: $75,000 – $105,000 / yr (or $40 – $55/hr W2) for on-site US contractor placements.
Target Client Organizations in Region
• Regional Enterprise Hubs: Vanguard ($8T AUM, Malvern PA), SAP America (Newtown Square PA), Comcast Tech (Philadelphia), AmerisourceBergen / Cencora (Conshohocken PA), IBX.
• Engagement Types: Staff augmentation, outsourced module development, custom enterprise web portals, and API orchestration.
• Direct vs Vendor Pay Gap: Direct Full-Time employees earn $160k–$250k+ TC vs $75k–$105k on third-party vendor payroll.
Stepping Stone & Direct Conversion Strategy
• Stage 1 (US Footing): Leverage vendor placement to enter the US market, build local on-site client experience, and establish SSN/credit history.
• Stage 2 (Direct Conversion): Transition from third-party vendor payroll to direct hire FTE ($160k–$240k+) or Tier-1 tech sponsor.
• Interview Focus: Clean Python/FastAPI/Django, REST API performance, SQL tuning, and clear client stakeholder communication.
